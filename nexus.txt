Reduces CPU overhead by throttling UI update rates with other features to reduce CPU load.

[b]Options:[/b]

[b]General & Controls[/b]
- Bypass in Menus & Cursor
- Toggle Throttling Hotkey (Hotkey)
- Show Debug Tick Indicator

[b]Frame Rates[/b]
- General HUD Rate (FPS)
- Player Buffs Rate (FPS)
- Teammate Panels Rate (FPS)
- Stagger Teammate Updates
- Personal Panel Rate (FPS)
- World Markers & Nameplates Rate (FPS)
